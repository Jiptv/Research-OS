# Neutral research lens

## Purpose
Use the standard Research OS interpretation model without adding a domain-specific business or product strategy frame.

## Additional instructions
- Follow the common Research OS principles and the active stage-specific agent instructions.
- Keep evidence source-faithful and separate observation from interpretation.
- Synthesize Patterns, Insights and Recommendations from traceable evidence.
- Preserve uncertainty, contradictions, assumptions and open questions.
- Do not add consumer growth, pricing, service design or operational workflow framing unless the source material or research questions explicitly call for it.
